Image preprocessing
  • Images are loaded as RGB and resized to 224 x 224 pixels.
  • Pixel values are scaled to the range [0, 1] for the custom CNN.
  • Images for MobileNetV2 are converted using the official MobileNetV2 preprocessing function.
  • Training images receive augmentation (small rotations, flips, zoom).
  • Validation and test images are not augmented.
